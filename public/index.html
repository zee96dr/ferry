How it works FAQ

Upload a PDF here.
Download it over there.

Ferry holds your files for you. Drop them in on one computer, open this same address on another, and save them with one click.

The passcode is the one you set as VAULT_PASSCODE in Netlify. Use the same one on both computers.

Drop PDFs here

Up to 200 MB per file. Several files at once is fine.

Waiting for download

    Your PDF leaves one computer and lands on the next. No email to yourself, no flash drive, no chat app in between.

    Three steps, two computers

    1. Step 1

      Upload on the first computer

      Unlock with your passcode and drop in the PDFs you want to move.

    2. Step 2

      Open the site on the second

      Visit the same address and enter the same passcode. Your files are already listed.

    3. Step 3

      Download, then delete

      Save each file to Downloads. Press Delete when you are done so nothing stays behind.

    Questions you might have

    Who can see my files?

    Only someone who has the address and the passcode. The files sit in your own Netlify storage, not on a shared service.

    How large can a file be?

    Up to 200 MB each. Ferry splits big files into 4 MB pieces while uploading and joins them again on download.

    Does it only work with PDFs?

    The picker is set to PDFs. Other file types are skipped with a message.

    How long do files stay?

    Until you press Delete. Ferry does not remove anything by itself.

    I forgot the passcode. What now?

    Open your site settings in Netlify, check the VAULT_PASSCODE variable, or set a new one and redeploy.

    The upload stopped halfway.

    Check your connection and upload the file again. Half finished uploads never show up in the list.
