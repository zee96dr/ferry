      Questions you might have

      Why do I need a print helper?

      Websites are not allowed to choose duplex, paper size or copies on your printer by themselves. A small helper program on the computer with the printer receives the job and prints it with those exact settings.

      What if the helper is not running?

      The job waits in the queue and prints as soon as the helper starts. You can also use the browser print dialog from the Print window.

      Does paper type change the printer tray?

      Only if you map each paper type to a printer preset in the helper config. Otherwise the type is shown in the helper console as a reminder to load that paper.

      Who can see my files?

      Only someone who has the address and the passcode. Files sit in your own Netlify storage.

      How large can a file be?

      Up to 200 MB each. Ferry splits big files into 4 MB pieces while uploading and joins them again on download or print.

      How long do files stay?

      Until you press Delete. Ferry does not remove anything by itself.
