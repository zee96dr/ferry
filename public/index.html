    Questions you might have

    Do I install anything on the shop computer?

    No. Ferry runs in the browser. Chrome or Edge gives the best results.

    Why does a print window still appear?

    Browsers never let a website print silently. Ferry prepares the exact sheets, sets the paper size, and opens that window. You only pick the printer and press Print.

    Can Ferry do double sided printing?

    Two ways. If the shop printer has duplex, choose "printer does it" and tick Two sided in the print window. If it does not, choose "by hand": Ferry prints the front sides first, then the back sides in the right order after you reload the paper.

    What about paper type and tray?

    Paper type and tray live in the printer settings, so Ferry cannot change them. Choose them in the print window or ask the shop. Paper size, orientation, copies, color, pages per sheet and margins are all handled by Ferry.

    Is it safe on a shop computer?

    The passcode is kept only until you close the tab. Press Lock and delete your files when you finish. Ferry also removes its temporary page images when you close the print window.

    How large can a file be?

    Up to 200 MB each. Very long PDFs take longer to prepare because each page is rendered before printing.
